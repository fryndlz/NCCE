# Product Vision

NCCE (Necromunda Campaign Chronicle Engine) turns a Necromunda tabletop campaign into an emergent persistent fiction.

Munda Manager already handles bookkeeping. NCCE should not compete with it. NCCE consumes mechanical state and asks: what does this mean to the people and places in the campaign?

The desired feeling is Dwarf Fortress / RimWorld meets X-COM applied to a real tabletop campaign. A fighter who begins as a stat line should accumulate scars, grudges, confidence, fear, relationships and a remembered personal history because of actual games. A territory should become a place because people fought over it. Repeated encounters should become rivalries. A gang's strategic rise or collapse should become a story. The entire campaign should develop a bird's-eye historical narrative.

The engine must preserve the distinction between what happened mechanically and what is fictional interpretation. It should be able to explain why a narrative conclusion exists by tracing it to campaign events.

C-NINE is the most mature authored example and should seed the system's quality bar, not constrain every gang to the same voice. Other gangs should develop their own voice and identity from faction, player-authored material, roster composition and play history.
