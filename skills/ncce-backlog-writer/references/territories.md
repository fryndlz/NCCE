# Territories

Read active form instructions for length/format; origins, events, scenes and histories have distinct purposes.

## territory_origin

Verified canonical function/type, actual identity, authored lore and compatible local fiction. Palanite precincts concern Helmawr's enforcers and authority, not an incidental bootmaker. Mines matter as extraction/work/value; shrines retain religious identity. Separate classification, designation and local name. Current ownership does not establish founder or historical holder. Distinctive layout/practice/background must not become another tally board, allocation system or jurisdiction joke. Preserve accepted origins unless replacement is requested.

## place_event

One recorded change with occurrence/observation distinction and historical holder. Keep it central; background is a lens. Changed separators/notes are not takeovers, custody orders or lived incidents. Unknown cause stays unknown. Credible local consequences without invented tabletop effects. Remarks must add a distinct interest/consequence rather than repeat the body.

## place_ambient

One invented local incident grounded in accepted place identity, relevant holder and supplied factual anchor. Mark connective_fiction; no mechanical effects or invented campaign fact. Unrelated people cannot displace the territory/institution. Compatible scenes can be mundane/comic/strange/disturbing; no mandatory secret or moral.

Virtual scene:TERRITORY_ID targets cover missing scenes. Preparing/saving creates no site entry; approved publication creates its real fictional slot. Existing scenes have real keys and require explicitly authorized generated replacement. Never invent database IDs or create mechanical ledger events.

## territory_history

Origin is labelled continuity; chronological factual events are evidence. Show development preserving holder and uncertainty at each stage. Optional scenes are not prerequisites or proof of events. Invented formative detail cannot become a dated campaign fact. Do not simply rewrite the origin.

## Checks

Verify context actually supplied, authority, compatible focus and repeated mechanisms across places. Other territories' fiction is not this territory's continuity. Historical events cannot inherit later outcomes as foreknowledge.
