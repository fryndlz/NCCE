# Owner standards recovered from the development conversation

These are initial direct-writing standards, not a replacement for the current category guidelines. Source: Blazej's instructions in the NCCE development conversation, compiled 3 November 2026. Newer explicit corrections take precedence within their scope. Do not mistake earlier assistant scores for owner approval.

- **Subject first.** A Palanite precinct story is about Helmawr's enforcers, their institution and its function. Both comparison drafts failed because a bootmaker displaced that subject. Unrelated invented people are not a substitute for a territory's identity or for campaign characters the owner cares about. This does not prohibit every minor unnamed person when they serve the actual subject.
- **Language.** Prefer direct, concrete, rougher/punk Necromunda prose closer to the manuals and official sources over fancy, convoluted or self-consciously literary phrasing. Consult verified relevant source passages; do not manufacture official quotes or copy long passages.
- **Remarks must earn their place.** The owner repeatedly found Administratum endings stilted, purposeless or artificially dramatic. A required remark should add a distinct, credible institutional consequence or interest, not a generic jurisdiction joke, repetition of the body or grand maxim. Do not use the C-NINE administrative voice for every faction or personal memory.
- **Meaning before obscurity.** Mine Workings A was liked; B was almost as good but too convoluted. Bone Shrine A was not understood; B provisionally passed except for its Administratum remark. These verdicts are useful labels, not permission to reconstruct the missing drafts or assert what exact wording they contained. Retrieve originals before treating them as examples.
- **Diversity concerns mechanisms.** Watch repeated tally boards, allocation/customary-ownership disputes, unofficial provision, ledger endings and maintenance/jurisdiction jokes. Renaming the props does not fix a repeated plot. Choose a compatible focus, not an arbitrary quota of themes.
- **Meaningful chronology.** A source correction or separator change is not a new lived event. No invented takeover, injury mechanism, purchase, recovery, casualty, attendance or political relationship.
- **Human effort.** Persist corrections and progress; do not require repeated copying, prompt pasting or round-by-round restarting. Escalations should be concise Problem / Solution / Risk with an actionable choice.

Keep objective facts, owner preferences and your editorial interpretations in distinct fields. Save accepted AND rejected examples with reasons when the original text/source identity is actually available. An accepted example transfers craft, not its fictional details.
