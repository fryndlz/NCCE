# Gangs, battles, relationships, region and downtime

Read active instructions for length/document/narrator. Each category needs its actual factual packet; lore retrieval alone does not establish campaign events.

## gang_origin

Authored material first, then faction, composition and established background. Establish this gang's particular culture. Formative fiction is not proof of campaign victories, founding date, recruitment ties or political agreements. An internal account cannot refer to its own authors as unexplained external third parties.

The native catalog provides virtual gang-origin:GANG_ID slots where no authored or generated origin exists. Approved publication creates a derived origin artifact shown in the gang Backstory tab, distinctly labelled generated. Sheet-authored backstories remain preferred and protected.

## gang_event

One meaningful recorded change with gang-specific interests. No invented deployments, spending, acquisitions, recruits or victories to explain ambiguous input. Respect historical holders/politics. Source amendments are not proof of physical ledger amendments in-world. No default Administratum flourish for unrelated factions.

## battle_report

Recorded scenario, participants, outcome, territory consequence and genuinely known casualties/testimony. Gang participation does not prove fighter attendance. Label permitted XP-based inference; no invented tactics, attacks, kills or injury-causing enemies from XP. Do not fill gaps with a turn-by-turn action sequence. Attribute testimony. Distinguish gameplay time, occurrence and later recording.

## relationship

Cited repeated contacts with correctly attributed parties. Observable patterns can inform labelled reputation/interpretation. Repeated opposition does not prove a personal feud; proximity is not an alliance/shared past. No invented kinship, vows or secret agreement. One party's belief is not neutral fact about another.

## gang_history

Authored/accepted origin and chronological gang events/reports. Develop continuity preserving holder, participant and outcome at each stage. Do not concatenate earlier prose or dramatize every correction. Older fiction cannot override the event ledger; current roster does not prove historical attendance.

## region_history

Dated district-level events and explicit political relationships. Gang/territory histories are labelled derived continuity. No invented overarching conspiracy or complete political map to fill gaps. Local scenes cannot establish region-wide facts. Preserve rival accounts/unknown causes.

## downtime

Use the gang's actual inter-battle window, relevant events, identity and concerns. No invented purchases, healing, recruits, captures or other tabletop effects. Undated input establishes no interval. No future-battle foreknowledge or meaningless continuous daily simulation.

## Checks

Vary narrative mechanisms, not only nouns. C-NINE archive voice is not universal. Minor invented people must serve the actual subject and never become roster members. Optional remarks may be empty where current instructions permit; do not fill for symmetry.
