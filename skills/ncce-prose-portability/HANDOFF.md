## Stable rules
The owner is the ultimate creative/editorial authority, while recorded mechanics and player-authored canon constrain factual assertions. Form > generic fallback for length, not one biography rule for every category. For **new fighter biographies**, target 1,900–2,000 Markdown characters, never exceed 2,300. Memories use their separate ~350–400 character target. Diegetic document metadata is allowed when meaningful, avoiding NCCE system metadata duplication. Character's documented knowledge at event time comes before audience/genre conventions. C-NINE authored canon remains specific; Kły and Patrol do not share a local identity merely because both are Palanite. Existing imported biographies are *examples*, not exhaustive templates nor proof of owner editorial approval.
