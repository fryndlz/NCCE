# Writing process — what runs today and what needs a writing agent

## Implemented in NCCE once deployed
NCCE retrieves campaign facts and authored context, resolves a source-labelled faction/gang/individual voice, attaches versioned form-sensitive literary guidance to **new** writer packets, and protects prior sealed requests from style revisions. A separate owner-reviewed proposal mechanism detects new gang/fighter backstory topics; it does not invent personalities or self-approve them.

## Human/connected-agent steps
The writing agent composes the literary work using the evidence, voice and literary context. The owner reviews the exact text and explicitly authorizes READY. The Exchange preserves WRITING drafts, and the relay imports READY text. This is a **manual editorial approval**, not automatic publication or guaranteed quality.

## Portable learning
A durable owner correction must be identified, scoped, written into versioned portable files, audited for conflicts, tested and repackaged. A chat cannot autonomously change another chat's attachments or update the ZIP without actively executing a file/repository operation. The latest ZIP must be retained by the owner or exported again from the deployed application. A policy update to GitHub does not retroactively modify already prepared packets.

## Form and identity
Fighter biography 1900–2000 characters target / 2300 cap; memory has separate target; other forms use their live form-specific contract. Character knowledge first; audience/genre shape expression. Palanite faction is not any particular player gang. Unapproved or missing player-gang profile stays neutral.
